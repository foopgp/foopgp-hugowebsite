{{- /* A row of phone screenshots, side by side on a wide screen and stacked on a phone, each under its own caption. Why not the built-in `figure` with a float: a portrait screenshot is tall and the blocks it illustrates are short, so two opposing floats strangle the text column and hang into what follows. And Bootstrap's width utilities are not responsive -- `w-25` is a quarter at every width, which made these 95 px wide on a phone, unreadable. Grid columns are responsive: one per row on a phone, where a screenshot has to be nearly full width to be read at all, and two or three side by side above that. Up to three images, named src1/alt1/cap1 … src3/alt3/cap3. `cols` overrides the column count on wide screens (Bootstrap's twelfths: 6 for two side by side, 4 for three). An image whose src is absent is simply not drawn, so the same shortcode takes one, two or three. */ -}} {{- $cols := .Get "cols" | default "6" -}}
{{- range $i := seq 1 3 }} {{- with $.Get (printf "src%d" $i) }} {{- end }} {{- end }}
