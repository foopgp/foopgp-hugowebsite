### Introduction to the White Paper: Rebuilding the Internet We Dreamed Of

The Internet, once a symbol of collective utopia promising freedom, equality, and knowledge sharing, has gradually transformed into a tool for surveillance, manipulation, and overconsumption. By 2024, it is evident that the original ideal has been hijacked by advertising agencies and economic models driven by relentless growth.

The white paper *Rebuilding the Internet We Dreamed Of* explores three key pillars to address this drift:  

1. Creating private digital spaces, shielded from data commodification and intrusive advertisements.  
2. Rethinking monetary and economic systems to deprioritize growth and reduce inequalities.  
3. Developing governance centered on the commons, inspired by the principles of open-source software and shared resources.  

This white paper proposes practical solutions for a fairer, more sustainable, and respectful Internet. Discover how OpenPGP technology and innovative economic models can transform how we live and collaborate in the digital world.
