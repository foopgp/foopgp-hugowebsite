## Demonstration: Securing an email with OpenPGP

This demonstration presents the basics of securing emails with OpenPGP:

- encrypting a message to ensure its confidentiality,
- decrypting a received email,
- signing a message to prove its authenticity,
- verifying an email signature.

The demonstration is performed using

[**Evolution**](https://help.gnome.org/evolution/intro-main-window.html) and [**GnuPG**](/about/openpgp/) for key management.

## OpenPGP-compatible email clients

- [Evolution](https://help.gnome.org/evolution/intro-main-window.html): Linux/Unix computers.
- [Thunderbird](https://www.thunderbird.net/): Linux/Unix, Windows, macOS, Android, and iOS.
- [K-9 Mail](https://k9mail.app/): Android smartphones.

## Use cases

- **Evolution / Thunderbird** + **GnuPG**: personal or professional use on a computer, with full functionality.
- **K-9 Mail / Thunderbird** + **OpenKeychain**: secure mobile use, ideal when traveling.

---

## Conclusion

**Evolution**, **Thunderbird**, and **K-9 Mail** are email applications that offer reliable OpenPGP support to secure your emails.

Depending on the platform used, these applications use **GnuPG** or **OpenKeychain** to encrypt, decrypt, sign, and verify messages, ensuring the confidentiality and authenticity of exchanges.
