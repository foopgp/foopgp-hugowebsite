### We are going to share our experience from our internship at the FOOPGP association.

---

We are two first-year students in a BTS SIO (Services Informatiques aux Organisations) program. This BTS is located in Gap. As part of our training, we must complete a mandatory internship to advance to the second year. We found this wonderful association that kindly hosted us for about a month (from May 27, 2024, to July 5, 2024). Here is our feedback on our experience:

During this internship, we worked with:

* GitHub
* Hugo
* Shell
* CodeBerg
* Dolibarr
* OpenPGP keys
* Evolution

Many of these applications and tools were new to us. This internship taught us a lot. We also learned how PGP keys work, which we found very interesting.

Now, here is our personal feedback on the internship:

Maël: PGP keys, which were previously unknown to me, were very appealing because they enhance security while simplifying it. The Go programming language also fascinated me due to its power and ease of use.

Evyn: During this internship, I was involved in various tasks, such as modifying the website using programming languages I was unfamiliar with, and also getting acquainted with PGP keys and related topics, like encrypted emails, all while using Debian, an operating system I had very little experience with.
