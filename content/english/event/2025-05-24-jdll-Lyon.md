**On the weekend of 24 & 25 May 2025**, the **foopgp** association was present at the 26th edition of the [Free Software Days](https://www.jdll.org/) in [Lyon](https://www.jdll.org/contact-and-informations).

---

On **Sunday (25 May) at 13:00** we gave a talk in **room D2.020** to explain **why tomorrow's world needs OpenPGP**.
- Link to the video: <https://videos-libr.es/w/vRzeAyKLvxf9EQhnhrw5xq>

---
