**On the weekend of 23 & 24 March 2024**, curious people or members of the **foopgp** association are invited to Pelleautier ( [imp. Serre des Isnards](https://www.openstreetmap.org/note/4144472#map=14/44.5146/6.0222&layers=N) ) and [online](https://meet.jit.si/foopgp):


### **Saturday 23 March**: Knowledge and intelligence sharing *("master classes")*

* **11:00:** Currency and global warming *(by [Jean-Jacques Brucker](../../author/jean-jacques-brucker/))*.
* **12:00:** Drinks + shared meal.
* **14:00:** Stakes of the *growth*, *tax* and *sharp* parameters of the
  association *(by [Jean-Jacques Brucker](../../author/jean-jacques-brucker/))*.
* **15:00:** [SuperNova](https://www.thunderbird.net/en-US/thunderbird/115.0/whatsnew/) news from the Thunderbird email client *(by [Laurent Céard](../../author/laurent-ceard/))*.
* **16:00:** Using OpenPGP with NitroKey/Yubikey *(by [Jean-Jacques Brucker](../../author/jean-jacques-brucker/))*.
* **17:00:** [DietPi](https://dietpi.com/) project *(by [Frederic Zwikel](../../author/fred-zwikel/))*.
* **17:30:** Drinks + games.

To help us plan Saturday, please [register **here**](https://www.qui-quoi.fr/evenements/DG4m8e53782Xy6WMYnEQ).

### **Sunday 24 March at 14:30**: General Meeting

* (minor) changes to the association's bylaws.
* improvements to the association's internal regulations.
* Activity report.
* Financial report.
* Choice of the parameters tied to our power tokens: *growth*, *tax* and *sharp*.

*Details and agenda of the general meeting:
