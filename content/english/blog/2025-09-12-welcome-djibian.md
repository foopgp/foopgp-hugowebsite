#### Discover

***We are delighted to welcome and introduce [djibian](/blog/2025-09-12-welcome-djibian/), the operating system that secures your data and respects your privacy***.

---

**Djibian** is a [Debian system](https://www.debian.org/index.html), that is to say a free and open source alternative to the Windows® or Apple® universes, into which we integrate or configure your current software, as well as the latest innovations to make it easier for you:

- Configuration of [your](/solutions/theme-identity/) [OpenPGP](/about/openpgp/) [security keys](/solutions/offer-security-keys/).

- Strong ***and*** easy-to-use [authentication](/solutions/theme-authentication/).

- [Signing](/solutions/signature/) and [encryption](/solutions/encryption/) of your documents or [emails](/solutions/theme-email/).

- For members: [participation](/solutions/theme-vote/) in [the governance](/about/rules-of-procedures/) of [the organization](/about/status/).

- For members: use of [governance tokens](/about/join/) as [exchange currency](/solutions/theme-currency/).

In addition, **djibian** has been designed to work equally well on recent machines and on older computers. It is therefore also ***an excellent choice if you want to extend the life of your computers by several years***.

---

Finally, if you help us grow, we will be able to complete and offer other innovative features, such as:

- Key on the keyboard to send a screenshot to a local support service.
- Automatic backup and recovery of your digital universe as soon as you plug your OpenPGP security key into a **djibian** system.
- etc.

---

#### Install

The latest ISO is available here: <http://iso.foopgp.org/djibian/latest>.

*Note: the default password of the default user:* ***foopgp****. To login, before creating real user with the command:*
```bash
$ sudo bl-djibian adduser --admin
```

If you want to install **djibian** yourself, we recommend using [Ventoy](https://www.ventoy.net).

Otherwise, we recommend contacting [our partners](/partner/).
