## Take back control of your data with OpenPGP security keys

**[Foopgp](/fr/about/)** is an organization that promotes and facilitates the use of solutions based on **[OpenPGP](/fr/about/openpgp/)**.

To this end, we have selected and developed tools that we have integrated into a Debian assembly (*Debian Blends*) called **[djibian](/fr/blog/2025-09-12-welcome-djibian/)**.

The workshop will allow you to discover our strategy for countering certain current dystopian trends, and to get hands-on with one of the solutions that helps you regain control over your data—which otherwise shapes our lives today.

---

### Workshop objectives

Starting from a freshly installed **[Djibian GNU/Linux](/fr/blog/2025-09-12-welcome-djibian/)** system, we will:

- Create a digital **[OpenPGP](/fr/about/openpgp/)** identity  
- Configure an **[OpenPGP security key](/fr/solutions/offer-security-keys/)** (e.g. *YubiKey*) to which this identity will be transferred  
- Use this key to:
  - Authenticate on the **[djibian](/fr/blog/2025-09-12-welcome-djibian/)** system as well as on various services (*SSH, Git*)
  - Sign documents and emails
  - Encrypt and decrypt documents and emails
  - Verify the signature of documents or emails
- **Bonus**: carry out a transaction in **[djis (Ɉ)](/fr/about/join/)**
