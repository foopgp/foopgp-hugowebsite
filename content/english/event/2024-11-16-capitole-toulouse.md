**On the weekend of 16 & 17 November 2024**, the **foopgp** association was present in the association village at [Capitole du Libre](https://capitoledulibre.org/) in [Toulouse](https://capitoledulibre.org/venir/).

---

This was an opportunity to:
- meet us
- create a few OpenPGP digital identities
- learn how to use security keys such as the Yubikey or Nitrokey
- discover and buy books related to our activity:
