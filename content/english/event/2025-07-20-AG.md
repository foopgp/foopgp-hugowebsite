The **foopgp** association convened its members for an Extraordinary General Meeting, **on the afternoon of Sunday 20 July 2025**, in Pelleautier[^address] and by videoconference on <https://meet.jit.si/foopgp>.

***[Minutes of the Extraordinary General Meeting of 20 July 2025](/assembly/2025-07-20-report-age/)***

*Note: in case of major issues with the meet.jit.si videoconference link, fall back to our weekly videoconference link: <https://cloud.foopgp.org/call/4du5irxe>.*

[^address]: At the registered office: 75 Impasse Serre des Isnards, 05000 Pelleautier.

### Programme

**14:00**: Welcome, coffee, greetings.

**14:30**: General Meeting.

**End**: We hope to wrap up before 16:00.

### Agenda

- A quick round of introductions.

- Introduction of Katia, candidate for the role of co-treasurer.

- Financial Report, [see the 2024 report](/documents/fr/20250619-foopgp-RapportFinancier2024.pdf).

- Vote: confirmation of Katia as the new co-treasurer.

- Presentation of the proposed amendments to the bylaws and internal regulations.
  - Comparison between [the current bylaws](https://foopgp.org/fr/about/status/) and [the draft](https://foopgp.org/fr/about/status-wip/): <https://foopgp.org/fr/about/status.diff.html>
  - Comparison between [the current internal regulations](https://foopgp.org/fr/about/rules-of-procedures/) and [the draft](https://foopgp.org/fr/about/rules-of-procedures-wip/): <https://foopgp.org/fr/about/rules-of-procedures.diff.html>

- Vote: approval of the new bylaws and internal regulations.
