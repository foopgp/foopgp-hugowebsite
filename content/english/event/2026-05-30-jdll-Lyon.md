**On the weekend of 30 & 31 May 2026**, the **foopgp** association will be at the 27th edition of the [Journées du logiciel libre](https://www.jdll.org/) (Free Software Open Days) in [Lyon](https://www.jdll.org/contact-and-informations).

---

### Our programme on Saturday 30 May

Two back-to-back sessions in the late morning:

- **11:00 — Talk (55 min) — *room Conf 3 - D2.018*** : *« Djibian: take charge of your digital future with OpenPGP. »* — an overview of foopgp's mission and innovations (free currency, OpenPGP ID, Djibian).
  → <https://pretalx.jdll.org/jdll2026/talk/KHTKUN/>

- **12:00 — Workshop (55 min) — *room Atelier 2 - D2.115*** : *« Secure your data with OpenPGP security keys (YubiKey, NitroKey). »* — bring your OpenPGP ID to life and leave with your YubiKey or NitroKey configured.
  → <https://pretalx.jdll.org/jdll2026/talk/8BQAHR/>

---

### At the booth, both days

As in 2025, you will be able to:

- Meet us.
- Join the association and receive your security key (YubiKey or NitroKey) as a gift.
- Discover and buy books related to our work, for instance:
    - [Internet : ce qui nous échappe / Coline TISON](https://www.babelio.com/livres/Tison-Internet--ce-qui-nous-echappe/799080) — *the hidden side of the internet: energy pollution, the data economy, the illusion of "free".*
    - [L’Homme post-numérique / François de Bernard](https://www.babelio.com/livres/Bernard-Lhomme-post-numerique/843005) — *an essay on the surveillance society and pathways for citizen resistance (foreword by Federico Mayor Zaragoza).*
    - [Climat, crises : comment transformer nos territoires / The Shift Project](https://theshiftproject.org/publications/climat-crises-transformer-nos-territoires/) — *a toolkit for local actors: six territory types, crisis scenarios, transformations to undertake before the end of the municipal mandate.*
    - [Vers la résilience des territoires / The Shift Project](https://theshiftproject.org/publications/vers-resilience-territoires/) — *three-part guide for the ecological transition: understand, act, renew local governance.*
    - [Vers la résilience alimentaire / Les Greniers d’Abondance](https://resiliencealimentaire.org/) — *eighteen months of investigation into the vulnerabilities of the French food system, with concrete tools for territories.*
    - [Effondrement : 20 scénarios possibles](https://www.yvesmichel.org/accueil/6833-effondrement-20-scenarios-possibles-9782364292574.html) — *twenty prospective narratives on possible collapse and bifurcation futures.*
    - [Revenu de base : un outil pour construire le XXIe siècle](https://www.revenudebase.info/2016/09/09/mfrb-publie-deux-livres-revenu-de-base/) — *a collective work by the MFRB (coord. Jean-Eric Hyafil): the case for an unconditional basic income.*
