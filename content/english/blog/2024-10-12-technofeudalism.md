Just as we were starting to see today's capitalism as [a kind of corporate
feudalism](//brewminate.com/the-feudal-model-in-social-analysis-from-medieval-europe-to-contemporary-america/),
it seems that view is already out of date.

Indeed, [Yanis
Varoufakis](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yanis_Varoufakis),
[Cédric Durand](//fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/C%C3%A9dric_Durand), [Razmig
Keucheyan](//fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Razmig_Keucheyan), and others
are reading the socio-economic situation in a way that strikingly echoes
the analysis we lay out in [our
white paper](//foopgp.org/blog/2024-08-15-whitepaper-part1/):

{{< video src="/largefiles/videos/blast/LE CAPITALISME EST DEJA MORT.mp4" type="video/mp4" >}}

Note that while a growing number of us[^others] can see that the current
economic system is driving us straight into a wall — with the underlying risk
of nothing less than the end of humanity — the foopgp organisation is one of
the few proposing [a fundamental and relevant part of the
solution](//foopgp.org/blog/2024-09-01-whitepaper-part2/).
